VATSIM Gate Finder v15 - occupancy fix

This version uses the official VATSIM public data feed for live aircraft positions.
The feed is refreshed by VATSIM about every 15 seconds.

Occupancy logic:
- parked/standing aircraft (0-2 kt): up to 125 m from stand coordinate
- very slow aircraft (2-5 kt): up to 95 m
- taxiing slowly (5-12 kt): up to 60 m
- faster taxiing (12-25 kt): up to 35 m
- >25 kt: never assigned to a parking position
- filed flight plans must have this airport as departure or arrival
- pilots without a flight plan are only considered when completely stationary
- one aircraft can occupy only one stand and one stand can be occupied by one aircraft

The official VATSIM feed contains latitude, longitude, altitude, groundspeed and flight-plan data.
